5 · What to test (per phone)

  1. Screen on, app open → alarm rings, notification appears.
  2. Screen locked → the full-screen alarm turns the screen on and rings.
  3. Swipe the app away from recents, lock the phone → still rings.
  4. Unplugged, screen off for 30+ minutes (Doze) → rings on time.
  5. Battery saver ON, then Do Not Disturb ON, then silent/vibrate mode → still rings.
  6. Bluetooth headphones connected, phone volume low → rings loudly.
  7. Schedule 10 minutes out, reboot the phone → alarm still rings (log shows “rescheduled”).
  8. Tap “I'M AWAKE” → silence, then the second check rings 2 minutes later. Hold 3 s → “confirmed”.
  9. Do nothing → rings until the 10-minute limit (“ring_timeout”).
  10. Force-stop the app in Settings → will NOT ring (Android cancels it). The server catches this case.

Write down for each phone: model, Android version, which steps failed. Xiaomi / Oppo / Vivo / Realme / OnePlus usually need autostart + “no restrictions” battery mode.
